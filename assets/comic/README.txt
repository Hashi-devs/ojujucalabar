COMIC ART FOR OJUJU CALABAR
===========================
Every cut scene is a comic strip. Each panel has an art id. Until you add a drawing, the game draws a placeholder.

To use your own drawing for a panel:
  1. Save it as  <art id>.png  (or .jpg / .webp) in this folder, e.g.  op_lagos.png
  2. Rebuild (python3 build3d.py). The build shrinks it and puts it in the game.

Tips
  - Panels are wide-ish: about 4:3 works everywhere. 1200 x 900 px is plenty.
  - Leave the top or bottom corners fairly plain: the speech balloons sit on top of the art.
  - Do NOT draw the words. The game adds captions and balloons, so they can be translated and voiced.
