Ojuju Calabar: privacy policy

Muniac Studios · last updated October 2026

The short version

The game does not collect or sell personal information. Your save stays on your phone. The only thing that leaves it is your speedrun, and only when you play in Haunted mode: the times you reach each room, and, if you choose to post it, your finishing time with a name you type in.

What is stored, and where

  • Your saved game (which room you are in, what you have found, your settings) is kept in your phone's own storage for this app.
  • There are no accounts, no tracking and no analytics.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes your save.

The speedrun leaderboard

In Haunted mode, while you are online, the game tells our leaderboard server (run on Supabase, in London) when a run starts and when you reach each room, so it can check your time is real. It sends the game's clock and a random run number, nothing about you or your phone.

At the end you can type a name and post your time. The board then shows that name, your best time, how many memory pages you found, and when. Use a nickname, not your real name. The game also keeps a random secret on your phone so nobody else can post under your name; only a scrambled copy of it is stored on the server.

If you never post, no name is ever sent. To have your name removed from the board, contact us through "Send feedback".

Fonts

The body font, Bricolage Grotesque, is loaded from Google Fonts the first time you open the game with a connection, so Google receives a normal web request for the font file. The Ojuju font is built into the game.

Sharing your result

If you tap "Share your escape", the game makes a picture on your phone and hands it to your phone's share menu. You choose where it goes.

Sending feedback

If you tap "Send feedback", your email or WhatsApp app opens with a message for you to write and send. We only see what you choose to send.

Ads

This version has no ads. If ads are added in a future version, this page will be updated first to explain what the ad provider collects and how to opt out.

Children

Ojuju Calabar is a horror game with jump scares, made for teenagers and adults. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Contact

Questions: contact Muniac Studios through the "Send feedback" button in the game's menu.
