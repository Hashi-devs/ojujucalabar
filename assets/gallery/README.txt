GALLERY PICTURES FOR OJUJU CALABAR
==================================
The gallery has seven pictures. Each cowrie a player has ever found unlocks one more.
Until you add a drawing, the game shows a placeholder.

To use your own picture, save it in this folder with the id as its name, then rebuild (python3 build3d.py):

  g1.png   Adeleke House             (the house on the hill)
  g2.png   Ojuju Calabar             (character design)
  g3.png   Adaeze Adeleke, Mama
  g4.png   Small Tobi
  g5.png   The garden, 1974          (Papa, Mama, Effiong with small Tobi on his shoulders)
  g6.png   Ekpo                      (the masquerade tradition that inspired Ojuju)
  g7.png   From Muniac Studios       (credits or a thank-you picture)

.jpg and .webp work too. Pictures are shown tall, about 200 x 260 in shape (for example 1000 x 1300 px).
The titles and captions are in src/extras.js (GALLERY), if you want to change the words.
